About

I am a Mathematics Education graduate and Mastercard Foundation Scholar currently pursuing graduate studies in Data Science at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS), Cameroon. My work sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, computational modelling, and education systems. I am particularly interested in using data-driven methods, machine learning, and mathematical modelling to address real-world challenges in public health, climate resilience, and educational equity across Africa. My long-term goal is to contribute to evidence-based solutions that strengthen learning systems and improve outcomes for underserved communities.

ML Finance Learning Platform

Overview: Designed and developed a comprehensive interactive web application for teaching Network Analysis and A/B Testing in Finance. Built to transform advanced machine learning course notes into engaging, simulation-driven learning experiences.

Key Features:

  • 100-question adaptive quiz system with randomized questions (Fisher-Yates algorithm)
  • Interactive D3.js network visualizations with real-time centrality metrics
  • A/B testing simulator with statistical validation (z-tests, p-values, confidence intervals)
