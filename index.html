I work on evaluation frameworks for large language models, with focus on cultural alignment in low-resource African languages.

I design benchmarks, analyze cross-lingual transfer, and combine human evaluation with LLM-as-a-judge. I'm interested in questions about what cultural knowledge means for language models, how they apply it to show cultural intelligence as well as how they attain cultural alignment, and how we measure it. How do we make LLMs multilingual while genuinely promoting multiculturalism?

Research

Cultural Alignment Evaluation of Large Language Models: An Igbo Cultural Benchmark

Master's Thesis · AIMS Cameroon · In progress

CAB-Igbo: 588-item benchmark across six cultural domains. Testing 12 models under two prompting conditions. Combining automated metrics, LLM-as-judge, and human evaluation to assess cultural appropriateness, factual accuracy, linguistic quality, epistemic fidelity, representation richness, and pragmatic proficiency.

Supervised by Dr. Shamsuddeen H. Muhammad (Google DeepMind Academic Fellow, Imperial College London)

Cultural Alignment Evaluation in LLMs: A Survey

Survey Paper · Under Review · 2026

Systematic review of 28 studies on African LLM evaluation. Developed taxonomy of benchmarks, tasks, and evaluation methodologies. Identified gaps in cultural grounding and semantic metrics.

Machine Translation for Lingala and Tigrinya

NLP Research · AIMS Cameroon

Fine-tuned mBART-50 for Lingala–French and Tigrinya–English, establishing first baselines. BLEU 15.09 for Lingala–French leveraging Bantu morphology transfer. Analyzed cross-lingual transfer failure in Tigrinya–English (BLEU 3.52) due to script and morphological mismatch.
