About

I am a Master’s student in Mathematical Sciences (Data Science) at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS), Cameroon. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Science Education (Mathematics) from Delta State University, Nigeria.

My academic interests lie in educational data science, statistical modeling, and the use of data-driven methods to understand learning outcomes and social inequality in Sub-Saharan Africa.
